Ryse: Son of Rome made me realize that life is too short to play mediocre games. No more. Great games only from here on.

This one could have been amazing. Design choices turned it into an extremely mediocre experience instead. As of this writing the game is on sale for $2.89. I think it is worth about a dollar, and that is only if you have already burned through every other game in your backlog. Harsh? Maybe. But it turned into a chore.

The only real positive is that it is short. Steam shows 8.3 hours played. Roughly spent 1 hour troubleshooting technical issues.

### A bad console port

The game has framerate problems. On my main machine (i7-12700K, RTX 3080 Ti, 2560×1440) I was stuck around 30 FPS. I run a 320 Hz monitor and wondered if that was causing the stutter. So I installed it on my retro PC (i7-4770K, GTX 1070 Ti, 1920×1200 @ 60 Hz). Same issue.

A bit of searching turned up some helpful fixes: a few configuration-file tweaks plus setting Low Latency Mode to Ultra in the Nvidia app. That resolved the problem. It ran fine on my machine machine. Classic hallmarks of a lazy console port. I also never figured out how to rebind controls on keyboard. The defaults feel like they were chosen by someone who has never played a PC game. Odd, coming from Crytek.

### Looks expensive, plays cheap

There are maybe five character models in the entire game and they get recycled constantly. You fight the same faces over and over. It gave me The Witcher vibes (that game was notorious for reusing NPCs).

Boss fights are beyond dumb. They drag on too long, the normal combat rules stop applying, and you spend most of the time trying to guess what the game wants you to do. It is usually something stupid. Zero room for creativity. The campaign stops just short of being fully on-rails. Invisible walls everywhere.

Strip out the unskippable cutscenes, the extremely repetitive combat, and the ridiculous padding, and you are left with maybe four hours of actual game. I cannot imagine paying full price for this at launch. Are console players really into games like this?

The story made no sense to me. That is not entirely the game's fault. I am bad at following plots in games. If the writing is not excellent, I lose interest. Not every game can be Deus Ex or Half-Life 2.

Disappointing. A bad PC port of a mediocre game. Not recommended.
