`Saturday, August 15, 2026, 3:26 PM CDT`

Going to play and stream some more. 

`Saturday, August 15, 2026, 5:55 PM CDT`

Finished the game. The final two missions are probably one of the worst levels I played in recent memory. Almost made me want to quit the game. I don't even know where to begin. After playing those, I feel like I know why this franchise died. Its hard to come back from this kind of a mess. What is unfortunate is that there is a fantastic game buried underneath the stupid design decisions. Instead of making an immersive shooter, they tried to appeal to achievement hunters and cover shooter fans on console. F.E.A.R was never that kind of a game. 

It could have been an all time classic, but as such it is a let down. 

I streamed the entire game for my friend. I had fun streaming, but I did not have fun playing the final two levels of the game. 

RIP F.E.A.R. There was potential for greatness, but the people making decisions had a terrible vision for the game. Sad.
