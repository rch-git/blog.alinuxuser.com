Planning for the build started in mid to early 2026. I was watching hardware trends, and things were not looking good. Waiting for prices to drop might not be a good idea because it looked like things are only going to get worse.

Broadly, there were two things I was certain of - 

- Air cooler instead of an AIO
- A full tower with no transparent side panel

#### An Expensive Day

I was supposed to take my KCNA exam in April. The plan was to order the build after I was done with the exam. That fell through due to unforeseen circumstances, I had to postpone the exam to July 26, 2026. I passed the exam on that day, so I went ahead with ordering the build. 

I ordered the build on 7/29/2026. I had to split it between 3 retailers. GPU was available at my local Best Buy, so I ordered it online for a pick up the following day.

Ordered the case from NewEgg because shipping was taking way too long with Amazon. I did have to pay for shipping. 

Case and the GPU were acquired by August 1, 2026. Getting the rest of the parts from Amazon seemed more of a hassle. I received an email informing that I was going to receive the parts on August 2, 2026, whic was earlier than expected. Later that evening, I received an email indicating that there was an issue with the shipment, and it could take up to August 5, 2026 for the parts to arrive. This was disappointing because I was hoping to build the machine over the weekend. 

Fortunately, all the parts arrived on August 3, 2026. We started building the machine after work that day. 

#### Building in Fractal Design Pop XL Full Tower

Building in this case was a good experience. There is plenty of room for the components. Pretty sure this cause would fit Noctua air coolers without any issues. There was plenty of clearance for both the Thermalright Peerless Assassin (140mm fans) and be quiet! Pure Rock Pro 3 (120mm). In addition to this cable management is also easy to accomplish. The case has 4 fans - 3 in the first, and one exhaust in the back. You can daisy chain the first fans such that they only use one fan header on the motherboard. 

I will never got back to a mid tower again. This case deserves a lot of credit. It might not look like anything special from the outside, but it excels where it matters - build quality, and ease of use.

For anyone that does not care about RGB and a transparent side panel, this case is a very strong recommendation. 

#### Cooler Troubles

We got the build complete around 10:30PM on the night of August 3, 2026. I ran Prime95, and once it hit 100% load on every core, the temperature soon each 104C, which is the TJ max for the CPU. I did not want to tackle this because I was tired, so I decided to revisit this after work the next day. 

The following day, I ran more benchmarks, and also tested real world scenarios. I typically have a Linux VM (for creating content for my blog) and a Windows VM (for work) running when I am playing games. So I powered on both the VMs, and while they were idling, I started Battlefield 6, and much to my surprise, this made the CPU hit TJ max. I know something was not right. 

I looked at the orientation of the cooler - the fans were facing downward, and the bottom fan was very close to the GPU plate. I wanted to flip the orientation of the cooler and test it. While I did not think this would be a game changer, it was worth trying before I purchased another cooler.

As I suspected, this did not make any difference. The performance remained poor under any kind of load. It was evident that the cooler is just not able to keep up with the CPU. 

I reached a point where I was getting tired of having to move the PC from one location to another to perform build changes, and testing. So I wanted to get it over with. I decided to buy the Thermalright Peerless Assassin and return the be quiet! Pure Rock Pro 3 Black. I contemplated buying the Thermalright cooler on Amazon. As I was doing that, an idea occurred to me. 

I was going to make a trip to downtown Dallas, TX on 8/8/2026 to apply for the commemorative US passport. I decided to go to the Micro Center that is located north of downtown in Dallas to pick up the cooler. This way, I will get to visit Micro Center for the first, and also pickup the cooler and test it that day. The plan was set in motion. 

##### August 8, 2026

The day to make the trip to Dallas was up on us. The details of the trip to Dallas can be found {{< newtab href="/posts/2026/08/2026-08-08-commemorative-passport/" text="<span class='link-color'>HERE</span>" >}}. 

After we picked up the passport, we made our way to the Micro Center. It was an interesting experience. Its like a super market for tech with a focus on PC components. As much as I love that this retail store exists, but RAM and GPU are horribly overpriced. Its plainly better to buy them online. We spend some time browsing the store. The keyboard section is very interesting. I wanted to pick up a cheap membrane keyboard, but I decided against it. 

Instead I picked up the Noctua NT-H1 thermal compound, and another cheaper thermal compound in case I needed it for my other machines at home. Picking up the Noctua thermal compound was a good decision because I ran out of the one I had at home, so I ended up having to use it. 

The total was $75.74. We checked out, and left the store. We stopped at In-N-Out on the way back. It was ~5PM by the time we got back home. We decided to get started on the build right away. 

I changed, and picked up the machine from my office and moved it to the dining area. We removed the existing cooler and swapped it with the Thermalright cooler. 

RAM and GPU are I returned be quiet! Pure Rock Pro 3 Black CPU Air Cooler to Amazon because the cooler is simply not good enough to keep up with Core Ultra 7 270K Plus CPU. Temperatures would hit TJ Max at 105C when playing Battlefield 6, and I have a VM running in the background, that is mostly idling. Video rendering would also push the temperatures to TJ Max. 

I happened to be in Dallas that weekend, so went to Micro Center to pick up the new cooler. 

Performance of the build in general is excellent. PNY 5080 is excellent. Special mention goes to the case. Its a no frills case with plenty of room. Thoroughly recommend this case. The only issue is that the front panel is solid. I wish it had a mesh so facilitate better airflow.
