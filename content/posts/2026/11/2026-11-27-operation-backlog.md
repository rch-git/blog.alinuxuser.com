I recently purchased two games:

- Ghost of Tsushima (Steam) - $29.99
- Shadow Gambit: The Cursed Crew (Steam) - $7.99

Not a very smart move, considering I already have an enormous backlog. Between our two libraries, we own more than 2,000 games. It took me 26 years to finish 185 games, which works out to roughly seven games per year. At that pace, it would take approximately 285 years to finish everything. Clearly, that is not going to happen.

I am not getting any younger, and I do not know how much longer I will continue playing PC games. The finite nature of life has become increasingly apparent to me. Because of that, I want to be far more intentional about the games I choose to spend my time on. I have decided to dedicate the remainder of 2026 and all of 2027 to working through my backlog and not purchasing any new games.

Realistically, I will not be able to finish every game in my curated backlog. Some of these games are quite old, and if I reach a point where a game becomes too boring or is plagued by technical issues, I will retire it. That said, I intend to give every game a fair chance before making that decision. I can already tell that some titles are going to be a slog. There is at least one Souls-like game on the list, and a few others may turn out to be technical disasters.

I do not plan on writing reviews unless I feel particularly strongly about a game or happen to be traveling and have some downtime to work on one from my laptop. I am already working on several articles, and those will take priority over reviews of 10-year-old games. In other words, articles that no one is going to read will take precedence over reviews of 10-year-old games that no one is going to read.

Instead, I am going to keep a game diary. It will essentially be a brain dump: unfiltered, loosely organized, and often written off the cuff. I will probably repeat myself from time to time. Most importantly, it will serve as a log of every gaming session. I may eventually have Grok Build process the markdown files to clean up the grammar and improve readability without changing the content or tone. Informal writing is useful for capturing thoughts quickly, but it is not always pleasant for others to read. You can read existing game diaries here - 

- https://blog.alinuxuser.com/tags/game-diary/

I will provide ongoing updates on the following platforms:

- https://infinitebacklog.net/users/weekendgamer
- https://x.com/WeekendGamerTX

I will also update HowLongToBeat with the hours I put into games that can be tracked through a launcher such as Steam or GOG.
