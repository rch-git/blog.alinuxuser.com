`Apr 18, 2026 Saturday 09:41:12 PM CDT`

Okay, I am about to start playing now.

The game has some technical issues. I found a YouTube video with a fix. It freezes when running in DX11 mode.

Access the following file:
`D:\SteamLibrary\steamapps\common\Batman Arkham Origins\SinglePlayer\BMGame\Config\BmEngine.ini`

Add or edit the options to the following:

```
AllowD3D10=True
AllowD3D11=False
```

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MtCbJ0B7IF8
