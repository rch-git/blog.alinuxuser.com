I purchased two games recently - 

- Ghost of Tsushima - Steam - $29.99 (Steam)
- Shadow Gambit: The Cursed Crew - $7.99 (Steam)

Not a very smart move considering I have so many games in my backlog. Between both our libraries, we have over 1500+ games. It took 26 years for me to finish 185 games - thats about 7 games a year. At that rate, it will take _214 years_ to finish all the games. Clearly this is not going to happen.

I am not getting any younger, and I dont know how long I will be playing PC games. The finite nature of life has become very clear. Therefore I want to be very intentional about the games I am playing. I decided to ear mark the remainder of 2026 and the whole of 2027 with completing the games in my backlog, and not buying any other games.

I will not be able to finish all the games in my curated backlog, especially because some of the games are older, and if I get to a point where the game is either too boring or if it has too many technical issues, I am going to retire the game. I will give each game a fair shot before I retire it though.
