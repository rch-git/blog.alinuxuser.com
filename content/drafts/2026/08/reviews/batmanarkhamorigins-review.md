This game was developed by WB Games Montreal, while Rocksteady was busy working on Arkham Knight. This game felt like a slightly buggy, less polished prequel of Arkham City.

Gameplay is very similar to Arkham City. There is a lot of side content in the game. I did not go through all that content. I did a couple of the side missions. I mainly stuck to the story missions.

The entire game is set during Christmas. I think it all happens over a period of 12 hours in the game universe. There are some great set pieces. Enjoyed those for sure. Combat is very much the same, but there are definitely some sections where the game is buggy. I had to play this in DX10 mode.

Boss fights are kind of a hit and miss for me. The bane boss fight especially is boring, because you get to play it multiple times. This is just pure gameplay padding. 

Story was interesting. This game focuses on when Batman meets Joker for the first time.
