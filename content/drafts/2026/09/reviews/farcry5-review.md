_I finished this game on `Sunday, September 6, 2026, 7:02:00 PM`_

I started playing this shortly after I finished Far Cry 4. 

Game world is the best in the series. They did a fantastic job recreating Montana. I want to visit Montana now. There are parts of the game that look photorealistic. Almost on par with Red Dead Redemption 2. When the day night cycle happens, the change can be rather dramatic. 

Mission design is super annoying at times. The game is split into 3 regions, for each of the siblings - the drug addict (Faith), the pastor (John), and the soldier (Jacob). When you reach a particular level of resistance, or in other words, annoyed the villain in the region to a certain point, you get ambushed and kidnapped, and the bad guy goes on some monologue - I am sure the game thinks its communicating something deep with me, but all I could see Ubisoft trying to recapture the magic of Vaas from Far Cry 3. They will never be able to do this. Vaas is a once in a generation kind of a character. It is not possible to manufacture another character like that. Everything they are doing in Far Car 5 feels boring and shallow. I tried to pay attention to Faith, and see if I could understand what the heck she is trying to tell me, but none of it made any sense. Following this, I started skipping every cut scene in the game. I feel like I didn't miss anything of value. The feeling I got is that you have some pretentious gender studies major trying to communicate something profound, but it was lame. 

In addition to this, the boss fights are extremely repetitive. You go down a bunker, and fight a wave of enemies, and the game then makes you have to do something dumb to defeat the enemy, and it all ends in the bunker getting destroyed, and the citizens taking back control of the region. To me, this is the weakest part of the game. The overall main mission design is very weak. 

Joseph Seed is no Vaas, and heck is not even Pagan Minn.

There are missions where you have to rescue 20 citizens who are being robbed or kidnapped by the cult. And the other mission where you have to destroy a certain number of trucks and boats carrying bliss. These are lazy. They are done for padding gameplay time. Classic Ubislop. 

So we are supposed to believe that a cult took over an entire county in Montana, and the citizens there are powerless because obviously Montana is famously liberal and no one other than bad guys own guns (like Colorado), and when the cult is committing arguably war crimes like burning people alive, leaving bodies hanging on lamp posts like its Witcher 3, the word never got around the country? No feds? No national guard? Oh also, the Hope County is completely isolated from the rest of the world, but they somehow have choppers, planes, a supply chain for drug trafficking (who are the customers and how are they paying for the drugs?), they are somehow getting gasoline for their transportation, and all their trucks etc., are branded, all the while being completely isolated from the rest of world...its SO stupid.

The world is amazing though. Whoever designed the world is nothing like the people that designed the missions or programmed the AI. People that made the world are actually good at their job. Can't say this about the writers or whoever programmed the AI, which is completely stupid. 

I thought Montana is a beautiful. There are vistas in Far Cry 5 which are truly beautiful. This game released the same year as Red Dead Redemption 2, and beyond a certain shallow point, these two games are not in the same league at all. The attention to detail, the AI, storytelling, in RDR2 are in a completely different league. It makes Far Cry 5 look like it was made by amateurs.
